## End use case and scope

Ray asks Personal Lucy in the existing Telegram bot about his history, decisions, ideas, and how they have changed. Lucy draws on the full approved personal corpus, connects related discussions, distinguishes Ray's statements from assistant suggestions, uses the latest supported understanding, and gives source-linked answers. She distinguishes standing preferences and decisions from historical proposals without describing old evidence as newly reconfirmed or mistaking “last known” for “verified today.” She can say what she has not found instead of claiming that something never existed. Corrections and forgetting change what she uses in future answers while the authoritative source and deletion records retain their proper status.

For this plan, **full memory** means all eligible records in the ChatGPT export corpus that Ray approves for Personal Lucy, plus Raymond's Telegram turns captured after the bot handoff, through a recorded cutoff date. Every eligible record must have an ingestion outcome: processed, no durable memory extracted, excluded by Ray/scope, unsupported format, or failed for retry. It does **not** mean every utterance becomes a lasting fact. New turns and later exports need an incremental path so the corpus does not immediately become stale. Attachments, other accounts, desktop files, and the deleted Utopia Telegram archive are outside this initial definition unless Ray explicitly adds them.

The existing protected source archive, corrections, and deletion records remain authoritative. Hindsight is the searchable interpretation and reflection layer, not a replacement for that archive. Hindsight's model extraction is remote inference through the approved OpenRouter route even though its database and embeddings are self-hosted. The three gates below are delivery milestones, not review or approval rounds. Continue across them within authorized scope and budget; report and retry isolated failed batches while useful service and unrelated batches continue.

## Delivery gates

### 1. Get a small Hindsight-backed Telegram answer working

Apply revision 0074 through a short-lived private migration job, deploy the updated routine, and run one small synthetic retain/recall/reflect/correct/delete/restart probe. The temporary migration credential must be removed with the job. Import the prepared three source-linked, reviewed interpretations, then deploy the pinned Hermes/Hindsight gateway configuration with automatic retention still off. Disable the old special quoted-topic prefetch when switching providers. Ask Ray two or three real Telegram questions, including the previously failed Gate + Magician question **and an ordinary, unquoted question**. Check that Hindsight actually supplies the answer, retrieves both topics where requested, cites the right sources, and does not turn a historical statement into a present endorsement.

**Exit:** Ray can receive a useful, source-linked Hindsight answer in Telegram; the bot still captures evidence; a correction and deletion demonstrably stop stale/deleted content from being used. For this first release, Ray can request correction or forgetting in Telegram and an operator can apply it through the existing authoritative workflow; a new self-service action UI is not required. Lucy acknowledges the request as pending until the operator action and Hindsight propagation finish; she must not claim completion on handoff alone. Verify the change reaches Hindsight source documents **and** derived memories, summaries, or cached reflections that could enter later answers. If Hindsight cannot provide the needed source references or correction behavior, make only the smallest adapter or source-document change needed before bulk import. Do not build another retrieval engine.

### 2. Inventory and backfill the approved corpus

Count the eligible ChatGPT records from the protected local manifest/export and the captured Raymond Telegram records. Record a cutoff, exclusions, unsupported formats, and an estimated extraction cost from a small representative batch. Use the existing archive/import path and stable source IDs to feed Hindsight idempotent documents in bounded batches. Once the small Telegram integration works, use the **same importer** for two streams: newly captured authorized Raymond turns and the historical backlog. Start recent-turn ingestion alongside backfill so Lucy can keep learning while old material loads. Import the 32 reviewed interpretations as interpretations, while importing other approved source material as dated, attributed history rather than presumed current beliefs. When raw extraction and a reviewed interpretation concern the same source, the reviewed attribution, endorsement, and time judgment takes precedence in Lucy's answer context; raw text remains available as historical evidence. A later supported statement from Ray can supersede an older reviewed interpretation. Let Hindsight extract links and summaries; do not hand-edit every statement or require individual approval for every extracted fact. Keep the existing authoritative correction/deletion state in sync with corresponding Hindsight document replacement or removal. Pace backfill under the existing daily limit so ordinary Telegram use retains spending room; seek a specific larger one-time cap only if the inventory and sample show it is needed.

**Exit:** a count-only report accounts for every eligible record and every batch outcome; every eligible record is processed or explicitly excluded; source IDs can be traced from recalled memories; no excluded/deleted source remains retrievable. “Processed” means extraction finished, not merely that an API accepted submission. A record may legitimately yield no durable memory. Any material unsupported or failed records are listed as **partial backfill**, not full completion, until processed or Ray explicitly excludes them. Failed batches do not stop independent batches or the working bot. Do not claim full coverage merely because jobs completed.

### 3. Prove full-corpus usefulness and keep it current

With the full approved backfill in place, ask Ray a small set of representative Telegram questions: cross-topic connection, a changed view, a historically true but currently unverified claim, a correction/deletion, and an absent or ambiguous topic. Check answers and source links with Ray; fix only material failures in the live path. Continue the Gate 2 importer for newly captured Raymond turns and later approved exports, with the same IDs, budget, and correction/deletion propagation. Keep automatic Hindsight conversation retention off while that source-linked path supplies memories; revisit it only if the importer proves inadequate.

**Exit:** Ray can use Telegram against the full approved corpus, get useful qualified answers with traceable sources, and see new authorized history appear without a manual corpus rebuild. The coverage report and known limitations make clear what “full” includes and what it excludes.
